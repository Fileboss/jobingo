# A « Projets personnels » section, one line per project

## Question

The CV format knew Résumé, Expériences, Formation, Compétences and Loisirs; a « Projets personnels » section was kept as an unknown one, ignored by the app. Projects could be written like experiences (a title, then bullets) or as a flat list.

## Decision

« Projets personnels » is a known section: a flat list, one short line per project, like the skills or the hobbies. The model selects the lines that serve the offer.

## Why

Compact: it keeps to the one-page CV.
