# Profile links show as a logo and a username

## Question

The CV printed the LinkedIn and GitHub fields as written, often as full URLs. The identity had no personal website.

## Decision

On the CV, LinkedIn and GitHub show as their logo followed by the username alone, the link kept clickable in the PDF to the full profile. A personal website is an identity field too, shown without its scheme.

## Why

A full URL is noise on a one-page CV; the logo says what the name is.
