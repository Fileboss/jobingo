# The interface follows the "Classeur" direction

## Question

Which visual direction sets the tone of the application, starting with the CV editor. Three structures were prototyped: "Feuille" (one sheet on a desk, diagnostics in the margin), "Frise" (a career timeline as navigation) and "Classeur" (a binder of index cards). Frise was recommended.

## Decision

Classeur: a binder of bristol index cards with staggered tabs, one colour per section of the CV, written in fountain-pen blue ink. The section colours carry over line by line to the text mode, so that the two modes visibly show one document.
