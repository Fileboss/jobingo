# Adapted CVs belong to applications

## Question

An adapted CV was tied to an offer, and could be made from the offer page, right after pasting an offer, or from the « Adapter » screen with no offer stored at all.

## Decision

An adapted CV belongs to an application, and is made from the application's page only, even for a spontaneous application: it then draws on what is known, the role, the company and the notes on it. The offer page and the offer list no longer adapt a CV, and the « Adapter » screen leaves the menu; the diff screen stays, opened from the application. An application comes from an offer; one created by hand is a spontaneous application, and an offer is not pasted into it.

## Why

The CV is sent with an application: it belongs with it.
