# Defaults and behaviour of the CV page

## Question

Three choices the brief left open: the default options, whether the summary is always printed, and what an option does once it is no longer blocked.

## Decision

- By default the side panel and the hobbies are on, and the photo is off.
- The summary can be unticked, like any other item.
- An option that was on and became blocked, such as the photo after removing it, turns back on by itself once it is available again.
